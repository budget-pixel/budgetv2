# FY 2027 site and print budget number audit

Reviewed September 28, 2026. This review compares the canonical 133-page print budget, the site, and the public FY 2027 revenues, expenditures, and activity CSVs. It does not certify every account line in the source spreadsheet.

## Reconciled and annotated

| Presentation | FY 2027 Board amount | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Budget Change Summary | $11,086,280* | Board operating and personnel comparison before $1,705,000 of capital. |
| Expenditure Ledger | $12,391,280* | Includes Board capital; $400,000 contingency is listed separately under Other Uses. |
| Constitutional Officers and Board Office Ledger | $12,791,280* | Full Board office total, including capital and contingency. |

The published expenditures sheet currently places $222,541 more in the Board than the print budget and $222,541 less in two agency groups: $181,500 in the Non-Profit Funding Program and $41,041 in Statutory & Other. The countywide $345,223,508 total is unchanged. The site now shows named FY 2027 reconciliation lines, rather than assigning the difference to unsupported accounts or projects. This restores the print budget's Board and agency summary totals. The sheet itself still needs to be brought into agreement with the adopted print figures.

The print Budget Change Summary, Expenditure Ledger, and Constitutional Officers summary now mark the Board row with an asterisk and explain this scope crosswalk. The corresponding site pages also carry the note, and the Board page states the full $12,791,280 amount exactly.

The site's Property Tax Allocation page had also used the old 3.4347 rolled-back/proposal rate as the FY 2027 final rate, including in its tax calculator. It now uses the published final rate of **3.2500 mills**; 3.4347 remains labeled as the rolled-back rate.

## Additional conflicts resolved from the public source sheets

The public revenue sheet totals **$151,592,125** in countywide ad valorem revenue outside the separate Mosquito Control levy. Its interfund allocation also implies **$16,988,464** for Capital Projects, and its General Fund Balance Brought Forward row is **$8,047,270**. The print book had older values of $151,054,833, $16,451,172, and $8,584,562. The print Consolidated Budget Ledger, Revenue Ledger, Property Tax Allocation, Fund Financial Ledger, Budget in Brief, and resource overview now use the source figures. A $537,292 increase in current-year property-tax revenue and equal decrease in appropriated balance leave the **$345,223,508** net expenditure budget unchanged. Current-year net resources are **$323,964,332** and balance brought forward is **$21,259,176**.

The site's name-based activity overrides had moved the Clerk of Court, Mosquito Control, and other offices into different functions. The public activity sheet classifies expenditures by department code. The site now follows that sheet, and the print Expenditure Ledger's FY 2027 function summary and department detail were rebuilt to match the print Consolidated Budget Ledger:

## Remaining source-sheet maintenance

The published expenditures sheet still allocates **$222,541 too much to the Board**, **$181,500 too little to Non-Profit Funding**, and **$41,041 too little to Statutory & Other** compared with the final print totals. The site and print book show these as explicit final-budget reconciliations. A person with edit access to the underlying Google Sheet should update its approved account rows, then remove the temporary site reconciliation after verifying the sheet's countywide $345,223,508 total and all four group totals. The published CSV URL is read-only; this repository cannot correct those account rows.

The decision-record narrative states an **$8,584,562** revenue effect from the tentative millage reduction. That is a comparison with the higher-rate plan, while the source sheet's **$8,047,270** is the final General Fund balance appropriation after the full revenue schedule. The former should be checked against the hearing record before it is described as the final balance draw.
