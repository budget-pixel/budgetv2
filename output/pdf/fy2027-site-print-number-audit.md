Reviewed September 28, 2026. This review compares the canonical 133-page print budget, the site, and the public FY 2027 revenues, expenditures, and activity CSVs. It does not certify every account line in the source spreadsheet.

## Remaining source-sheet maintenance

The published expenditures sheet still allocates **$222,541 too much to the Board**, **$181,500 too little to Non-Profit Funding**, and **$41,041 too little to Statutory & Other** compared with the final print totals. The site and print book show these as explicit final-budget reconciliations. A person with edit access to the underlying Google Sheet should update its approved account rows, then remove the temporary site reconciliation after verifying the sheet's countywide $345,223,508 total and all four group totals. The published CSV URL is read-only; this repository cannot correct those account rows.

The October 2 source check found a second, directly visible conflict: the expenditures tab budgets **$268,500** for the Non-Profit Funding Program, while the revenues tab still assigns **$450,000** of ad valorem tax to it. The Property Tax Allocation page and print book keep the revenue-sheet amount so the $151,592,125 tax total remains reconciled, and disclose the $181,500 difference. Do not silently change the property-tax amount or reassign that difference to another recipient without an approved revenue-sheet update or explicit allocation decision.

The decision-record narrative states an **$8,584,562** revenue effect from the tentative millage reduction. That is a comparison with the higher-rate plan, while the source sheet's **$8,047,270** is the final General Fund balance appropriation after the full revenue schedule. The former should be checked against the hearing record before it is described as the final balance draw.

## October 1 adoption check

The County [announced adoption of the FY 2027 budget](https://www.mywaltonfl.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/3564) after its September 28 final hearing. The [final-budget resolution in the hearing packet](https://walton.civicweb.net/document/538081/) and the County announcement both state a **$345,223,508** countywide budget, including a **$1,429,937** North Walton Mosquito Control District budget. The [final-millage resolution in the packet](https://walton.civicweb.net/document/538072/) states **3.25 mills** countywide and **0.441 mills** for the district. These match the site's countywide budget and stated millage rates.

The site's **$1,426,937** Mosquito Control tax-card figure is explicitly labeled *Property Taxes @ 95%*. An October 1 check of the published [revenue CSV](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRc6KHhTwcdREn_SvLONy_cucXH8NxF45hgdyn8IoFGSeTbIVKtDGMMWsbgSFpMizxtxy_fE-pAMmiu/pub?gid=1812049672&single=true&output=csv) and [expenditure CSV](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRc6KHhTwcdREn_SvLONy_cucXH8NxF45hgdyn8IoFGSeTbIVKtDGMMWsbgSFpMizxtxy_fE-pAMmiu/pub?gid=0&single=true&output=csv) found that **all Fund 105 revenue rows total $1,426,937 and all Fund 105 expenditure rows also total $1,426,937**. Thus the $3,000 difference from the adopted district budget is a confirmed source-ledger gap, not just a comparison between tax revenue and a broader budget. No published Fund 105 account row identifies the missing $3,000. The Mosquito Control page now discloses this difference; the source owner must identify and correct the account allocation before Fund 105 can be fully reconciled.

The agenda-packet resolution is an unsigned copy. Obtain the executed resolution and check for any adopted amendments before production release; the County announcement confirms adoption but does not itself certify every account line.
As of October 1, the County's [budget publications page](https://www.mywaltonfl.gov/260/Budget-Publications) still lists the FY 2027 book as **Tentative** and does not link a FY 2027 final publication. Do not treat the tentative publication or unsigned packet attachment as the executed final source.

## Explorer-card scope

On October 1, the live explorer calculated $135,644,083 for Board departments, $148,896,886 for constitutional officers and the Board office, $51,277,454 for capital excluding Sheriff, and $9,158,467 for independent agencies. Those four displayed views sum to $344,976,890, which is $246,618 below the $345,223,508 countywide total. This is **not** a separate $246,618 source-ledger gap: the views have overlapping and different scopes (for example, the constitutional-officer total includes capital that is also included in the Capital view). Their independently rounded shares happen to add to 100.0%, which could imply an exact partition. The landing page now states that the views overlap and should not be added together. Any future four-part allocation should be built from a mutually exclusive account-level classification and reconciled to the adopted total before replacing these views.

## Department ledger visibility

The default budget-ledger view hides line items with a zero FY 2026 budget until the reader selects **View Prior Years**. Some of those lines have nonzero FY 2027 amounts; for example, the Solid Waste ledger includes $16,827,500 across 10 such lines. The total has always included these amounts, so the visible rows alone did not explain it. The site now states the hidden FY 2027 amount and line count beside each affected ledger. A browser check across all 94 content pages matched every hidden nonzero FY 2027 account amount to its ledger disclosure; the Solid Waste prior-years control was also tested end to end. Views without that control no longer hide these current-year rows solely because FY 2026 was zero.
