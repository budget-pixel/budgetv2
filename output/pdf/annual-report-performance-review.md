Comparability limitations and remaining measures:

- Building Construction: report lists 155 buildings cleaned/maintained, not the existing combined measure of facilities maintained and inspected. No work-order result is provided. Neither existing value was replaced.
- Building: January–July figures are partial-year results. Print labels identify that period and omit a full-year trend comparison.
- Code Compliance: report lists cases recorded through September 30 (734 street / 12,850 beach), rather than cases resolved. It does not give the voluntary-compliance rate. Neither existing measure was replaced.
- Administration: individual document categories do not establish a matching total for the existing BCC directives/task orders/agreements measure. The video figure is directly comparable.
- Eagle Springs pool: report page 31 labels 1,332 as 2025; page 17 calls the same figure summer 2024. Print carries a period warning and suppresses the trend.
- Grill: 32,723 guests served is not a count of guest checks. Existing guest-check series and its target remain.
- Emergency Management: no CERT volunteer-hour total provided.
- Engineering: report confirms five-year work-plan and website activity, consistent with existing Yes entries.
- Environmental Resources: report's petroleum inspections, lighting reviews, and projects are different measures from stormwater inspections and completed conservation projects.
- Extension: no corresponding section or measure totals found.
- GIS: activity narrative gives no matching assistance or map-production counts.
- HUD: average of 300 families is consistent; no voucher utilization rate is provided.
- Human Resources: 208 new hires/rehires is not employees onboarded and trained. 345 benefit changes is not the combined personnel-action/benefit-change measure. These were not substituted.
- Libraries: report separates more than 220,000 visitors from more than 38,000 program attendees; overlap is unknown. Existing combined visitor/attendee measure was not replaced by either figure or their sum.
- Mosquito: no numeric inspection total provided.
- Wastewater: no matching lift-station inspection/repair count provided.
- OMB: compliance narrative supports existing 100%; $74,787,904 grants managed and $10,971,257 reimbursed expenditures are different measures from total grant expenditures managed. No unsupported substitution made.
- Attorney: over 300 contracts/agreements plus 26 procurements cannot establish an exact combined count. 846 public-record requests is not response time in days.
- Planning: over 3,200 applications excluding short-term rentals and 8,600 short-term-rental applications are different scopes from the existing EnerGov permit measure. MS4 compliance narrative is consistent.
- Probation: report uses October 2024–September 2025, while original label says calendar year. Print identifies FY 2025 and suppresses the cross-period trend.
- Public Works: road-mile count corrected; project lists do not establish a directly comparable total for the existing capital-project measure.
- Purchasing: 30+ includes ITQs and ITNs as well as ITBs/RFPs/RFQs; print identifies the scope and suppresses a precise trend.
- Recreation: 4,214 is 1,180 basketball + 1,451 spring soccer + 1,228 fall soccer + 48 golf camp + 24 soccer camp + 59 basketball camp + 124 adult kickball. This is participation, not unique people; separately listed supported/travel programs are excluded. Trend suppressed because scope of earlier totals is unconfirmed.
- Soil Conservation: program description has no matching EQIP/CSP counts or acreage.
- Solid Waste: report gives no matching Class I tonnage or recycled-tonnage totals.
- Veterans: no exact outreach-event count provided.
- Tourism Administration: economic job/lodging-rate measures are not supplied in this report.
- Sales, Communications, Marketing: report supplies new activity counts, but these pages have no existing comparable performance series to correct. No targets or earlier-year results were invented.
- Beach Operations: historical access-point count uses 15 + 47 from the report's accomplishments section, rather than mixing that with its differently scoped general facility inventory.
