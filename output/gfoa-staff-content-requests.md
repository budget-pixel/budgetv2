# FY2027 tentative budget — information requested from County staff

Please provide approved facts and source documents, not estimates created solely to fill a publication requirement. Identify the owner and effective date for each response.

1. **County Administration / OMB:** Provide a 2–4 sentence explanation of each of the three largest community or organizational challenges influencing FY2027. For each, identify the funded response, its cost, the decision/tradeoff, expected result during FY2027, and measure of success.
2. **OMB / Solid Waste / Tax Collector:** Explain the expense-to-revenue display differences for Solid Waste ($17 million) and Tax Collector ($4,050,600). Identify the exact funding/financing rows and whether the display scopes should differ.
3. **HR / OMB:** Confirm the definitions and source note to publish beside the verified FY2027 totals of 1,515 FTE, 1,508 full-time positions and 7 part-time positions, including treatment of seasonal and vacant positions.
4. **HR / department budget owners:** Reconcile the position-cost roster to personnel appropriations. Explain department-level allocation adjustments and the 33 model exceptions, particularly Public Works, Beach Operations, Beach Tram, Code Compliance and County Attorney. Confirm which figures are estimates versus authorized position budgets.
5. **Every department:** Confirm the department's function, its core services, FY2027 service-level changes (or explicitly “no change”), reasons for material budget/staffing changes, the responsible official, and how results are reported to the public.
6. **Every department:** For each performance measure, provide the definition, data source, most recent actual, FY2027 target, reason for the target, reporting frequency and accountable owner. Explain the public benefit, not just the number of activities completed.
7. **Program owners / OMB:** Approve the eight service-area boundaries and contributing units. Provide actual program-generated revenue, cost allocation methodology, subsidy/fee policy, expected service levels, major changes and outcome measures. Distinguish tax/shared-revenue allocations from user-fee cost recovery.
8. **Revenue owners / OMB:** Provide the estimate method and major FY2027 assumptions for each significant revenue source. Confirm restriction, recurrence and degree-of-control classifications; identify one-time revenue and what it funds. Provide the source and assumptions for resident/visitor/user burden estimates.
9. **OMB / County Attorney:** Provide the adopted reserve/fund-balance policy, approval reference, applicable balance definitions and targets, and a comparison of the forecast to those targets. Explain future commitments, deferred expenses and current budget decisions affecting FY2028–2031.
10. **OMB / County Attorney:** Confirm the current property-tax amendment reference and supply an approved local risk scenario, assumptions, possible revenue effects and response options. Separate the existing-law tentative budget from contingent scenarios.
11. **Capital/project owners:** Reconcile the $71.3 million FY2027 project plan with annual capital appropriations, officer projects, grants, carryforward and equipment schedules. For each major project, provide purpose, owner, priority rationale, funding, total versus annual cost, dates/status, new/replacement/rehabilitation classification and recurring operating impact.
12. **Clerk / OMB:** Provide dates and direct agenda/recording links for all four public budget workshops; confirm that no public engagement occurred. Supply verified hearing notices, participation instructions, and the major decisions made. Do not attribute changes to public feedback if none was received.
13. **County webmaster / OMB:** Update the main County Budget Publications page's OpenGov description for FY2027. Identify the publication owner, approved “last updated” timestamp, refresh cadence, amendment log and historic-budget access policy.
14. **IT / privacy / accessibility owners:** Confirm deployed public-data permissions and redaction review; approve analytics/session-replay consent, masking and disclosure; complete real-device and assistive-technology testing; and arrange a correctly structured, reconciled PDF export. Identify translation and video-caption needs.
15. **OMB:** Confirm with GFOA the intended submission date and whether this application will use the revised 8/1/2025 framework available for applications beginning January 2027.
