The Fine & Forfeiture Fund or Sheiff Fund within Walton County’s Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) supports critical infrastructure and facility improvements related to public safety and law enforcement. This fund is financed through fines, forfeitures, and property taxes, ensuring that essential law enforcement services are maintained.

These investments focus on law enforcement facilities, emergency response infrastructure, technology enhancements, facility upgrades, and emergency preparedness initiatives that help Walton County provide a safe and secure environment for residents and visitors.
