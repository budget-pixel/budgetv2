Capital Improvement Plan

Transportation Fund Schedule

The Transportation Fund is a key component of Walton County’s Capital Improvement Plan (CIP), providing financial resources for the construction, maintenance, and repair of County roads and related infrastructure. The fund is supported by revenues from motor fuel taxes, the local option gas tax, state revenue sharing, inter-fund transfers from the Solid Waste and General Fund, and various grants designated for highway and road construction.
