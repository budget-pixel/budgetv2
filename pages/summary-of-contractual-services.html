About This Ledger

This ledger summarizes budgeted Contractual Services included in the adopted budget for departments managed by the Board of County Commissioners. Contractual Services include work performed by outside vendors and contractors to support County operations, such as professional services, maintenance, technology, engineering, and construction. The information is provided to increase transparency into planned contracted services at the time the budget was adopted.

This ledger is a snapshot of the adopted budget and is not intended to serve as a current listing of active County contracts.

What’s Included

This ledger includes contracted operating services funded in the adopted budget. Capital projects are not included; for information on capital projects, refer to the Capital Improvement Plan.

This ledger does not include contractual services managed by the Constitutional Officers (Sheriff, Clerk of Courts, Property Appraiser, Tax Collector, and Supervisor of Elections), the Medical Examiner, the Health Department, or other agencies funded by the County but not directly managed by the Board of County Commissioners.

Understanding the Information

The County budgets for services—not specific vendors. Vendor names, where shown, identify the current service provider under an existing agreement approved by the Board of County Commissioners and are provided for informational purposes only. Inclusion of a vendor does not constitute approval of funding for or the award, renewal, or extension of a contract to that vendor. Because contracts may be competitively procured, amended, renewed, or terminated throughout the fiscal year by the Board of County Commissioners, the information on this page may not reflect current contractual arrangements. Where no service provider is listed, the service is anticipated to be procured in the future or does not have an assigned vendor at the time the budget was adopted.

Budgeted amounts represent planned funding for the fiscal year and are estimates based on the best information available when the budget was developed. They do not necessarily represent the total value of a contract or the amount ultimately spent. Actual expenditures may vary based on procurement results, contract amendments, project scope, and operational needs.

All contracts are procured in accordance with County procurement policies and are approved solely by the Board of County Commissioners.
