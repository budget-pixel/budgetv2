The Tourist Development Fund is a critical component of Walton County’s Capital Improvement Plan (CIP), dedicated to enhancing tourism-related infrastructure, preserving natural resources, and improving visitor amenities. Funded by the local option tourist development tax collected from accommodations within designated tourist zones, the fund ensures that the county’s beaches, recreational areas, and tourism facilities remain world-class destinations.

Beyond marketing initiatives, this fund supports beach renourishment, public access improvements, multi-use paths, storm resiliency projects, and visitor amenities. These capital projects enhance the overall tourism experience while ensuring the sustainable development of Walton County’s coastal and recreational assets.
