Medical Examiner: The $469,768 decrease reflects facility funding included in FY 2026 that does not recur in FY 2027.

Board Department Total: Board department amounts in this schedule include operating and personnel only. Their capital spending is shown in the Capital section, so these amounts may be lower than the totals on individual department pages.

Board of County Commissioners: The amount here also excludes $1,705,000 of capital; the Expenditure Ledger reports the Board's $400,000 contingency separately under Other Uses.
