Search every public transaction Walton County has recorded since FY 2020 — vendor payments, fee receipts, and everything in between. Filter by vendor, description, amount, date, or fund and fund your own review of where the money actually went.

Reading this data: Department and fund show as their internal codes, not names, in this table — that crosswalk hasn’t been built yet. Amounts are recorded as positive for expenses and negative for revenue receipts (e.g. a permit fee), which is why the Type filter reads that way. This is transaction-level actuals, separate from the FY 2027 tentative budget figures shown elsewhere on this site.
