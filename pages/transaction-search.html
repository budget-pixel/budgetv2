How to read transactions

Choose a department by name below; the search automatically matches its underlying transaction codes. Expense credits include vendor refunds, expense reversals, and refunds of prior-year expenditures. They are separate from expenses and revenue receipts. Refunds or reversals in revenue accounts have their own label. Transaction types use both the account and the recorded amount's direction; a negative amount alone does not mean revenue. CSV downloads include the type and preserve the original signed amount. This is transaction-level actuals, separate from the FY 2027 final budget figures shown elsewhere on this site.
