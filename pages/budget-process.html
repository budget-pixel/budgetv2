FY 2027 departmental guidance

Departments were generally asked to reduce operating requests by 5%

— Calculating departmental operating changes

As departments prepared their FY 2027 budget requests, the County generally directed them to identify a 5% reduction in operating expenditures. This target was intended to encourage departments to review recurring costs, prioritize essential services, identify efficiencies, and distinguish core operating needs from items that could be reduced or deferred. The direction applied to operating-budget development and should not be read as an across-the-board reduction to every department, service, or expenditure category.
