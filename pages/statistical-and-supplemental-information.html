Principal Property Tax Payers

The table lists the top taxpayers in Walton County, highlighting their assessed property values and their contributions to the total net assessed value. Fl Power and Light leads with an assessed value of $167,818,558, contributing 0.36% to the total. Following are EBSCO Gulf Coast Development with $166,886,571 (0.36%) and Choctawhatchee Electric Cooperative with $158,087,812 (0.34%). The total assessed value of these listed top taxpayers is $932,394,393, which collectively accounts for 2.00% of the total net assessed value. This data underscores the significant financial contributions of key property owners to Walton County's tax base, supporting local services and infrastructure.
