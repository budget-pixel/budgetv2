Debt and Financial Forecast

Debt Ledger

Walton County’s only outstanding long-term debt is a $27,000,000 Note Payable issued in 2015 and a $2,500,000 Revenue Promissory Note issued in 2020, both used to fund public improvements — most notably the Broadband project. Both notes are repaid exclusively from the County’s half-cent sales tax, not property taxes, and are scheduled to be fully repaid by FY 2030.
