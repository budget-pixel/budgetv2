What the County currently owes

Walton County’s only outstanding long-term debt is a $27,000,000 Note Payable issued in 2015 and a $2,500,000 Revenue Promissory Note issued in 2020, both used to fund public improvements — most notably the Broadband project. FY2027 debt payments are scheduled to end in FY 2030.

Legal debt limit & credit rating

Florida sets no legal debt limit for counties (Florida Statute 200.181), and Walton County hasn’t adopted one internally — the County only takes on debt that reliable revenue can comfortably support. Independent rating agencies (Moody’s, Standard & Poor’s, and Fitch) assess how likely a government is to repay its debts, which affects a bond’s interest rate; Walton County holds a Aa1 rating from Moody’s Investors Service.
