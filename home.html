A simple overview

A budget is a plan for public money.

Each year, Walton County estimates the revenue it expects to receive and decides how those resources will support services, employees, facilities, infrastructure, and other community needs.

The budget must balance: planned spending cannot exceed the resources available. Some money may be used broadly, while restricted revenue must support a specific purpose.

How the explorers work

Start broad. Select what interests you. Open the ledger for detail.

You do not need to understand government accounting to use this site. The explorers begin with the biggest ideas and let you move into more detail only when you want it.

  1. 1
    Begin with the summary

    Use the large totals and short explanations to understand what the page is showing.

  2. 2
    Select a card or filter

    Choose a revenue, department, function, or project to focus the information.

  3. 3
    Open the ledger

    Review the complete table when you need exact amounts, prior-year comparisons, or budget-line detail.

Helpful tip: Hover over badges and question marks for plain-language definitions. Selecting and closing details will keep your place on the page.
