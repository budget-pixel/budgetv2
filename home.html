How the budget is organized

Walton County’s budget is organized into four main spending categories: Department Budgets, the operating and personnel cost for Board of County Commissioners departments; Constitutional Officers, the budgets for the six independently elected constitutional officers and their respective staff, capital, and operating needs; Capital, the Board of County Commissioners’ capital requests for machinery, roads, bridges, infrastructure, and parks; and Independent Agencies, entities the County is required to fund under state mandates, or agencies the County elects to fund.
